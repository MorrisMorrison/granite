# Install on your phone

Granite runs in your browser, and you can add it to your home screen. It then opens from its own icon, full screen, and keeps working when you have no signal.

Your server has to be reachable from your phone over HTTPS, because browsers only allow installing from a secure address. See [Self-hosting](../self-hosting/install.md) for how to set that up.

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open your Granite address in Safari.
2. Tap **Share** in the toolbar. In newer versions of Safari, tap **⋯** first.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.

## Android

1. Open your Granite address in Chrome.
2. Open the browser's menu **⋮**.
3. Choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

## Links opened inside other apps

Apps like Instagram or WhatsApp open links in their own built-in browser, which can't add pages to your home screen. Open the page in Safari or Chrome instead. Look for **Open in browser**, often behind **⋯** or **⋮**.

## Android app

A native Android app with a rest-timer alert that works with the screen locked is in development, but it isn't available as a release yet.
