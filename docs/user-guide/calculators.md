# Calculators

Open **Calculators** from the icons at the top of the screen. They use your weight unit from **Settings**.

- **Plates per side:** enter a total weight to see what to load on each side of the bar.
- **Estimated 1RM:** enter a weight and reps to estimate your one-rep max, with suggested weights for other rep targets.
- **Warm-up sets:** enter your working weight to get a warm-up ramp.
